## How this was verified, and the limitation you need to know

Direct page fetching was blocked in this environment: the egress proxy refused `HTTPS CONNECT` to
every external host tested (403), so `WebFetch` and `curl` were unusable for all organizer sites.
Verification was done instead by **search restricted to each organizer's own domain**, which surfaces
organizer-page content without going through round-up blogs.

What that means for you, concretely:

- **URLs in this document are organizer-owned domains that appeared as indexed results** for
  domain-restricted searches. They were not opened and rendered. Treat them as high-confidence but
  not click-tested.
- **Facts are drawn from organizer-site content** (rules PDFs, FAQ pages, deadline pages) except
  where a row explicitly says otherwise. Three rows lean on non-organizer sources and say so.
- Every row carries a confidence note separating *verified* from *inferred* from *not published yet*.
- Where a 2026–27 or 2027 figure genuinely is not yet published as of 18 Sep 2026, the row says so
  and gives the most recent prior-cycle figure as a **planning proxy, clearly labelled**. Do not put
  proxy dates on the live page.

A single human pass opening the URLs before publication would close the gap. The rows flagged
**LOW CONFIDENCE** should not go live without that pass.

### ❌ CUT — National History Day, for the 3–5 band

**National History Day is a strong 6–12 entry (see Grades 6–8) and should stay on the page there.
It should not be listed as a grades 3–5 competition.**

NHD has exactly **two national divisions: Junior (grades 6–8) and Senior (grades 9–12)**. There is no
national elementary division. Some state affiliates separately sponsor 4th/5th-grade participation,
and NHD notes that affiliate-sponsored 4th/5th-grade participation does not count against a student's
seven-year NHD maximum — but it is affiliate-dependent, not a national program, and we cannot make a
national claim about it on a parent-facing page.

**Recommendation:** list NHD under 6–8 and 9–12 only. If you want a 3–5 history option, write copy
that points parents to *their own affiliate* rather than to a division that doesn't exist nationally.
Verified on `nhd.org`.
